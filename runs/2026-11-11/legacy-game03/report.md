I won the game on dominion.games against the bot Lord Rattington, **41 VP to 40**. They took the last Province on turn 22. We each took 22 turns, so the 1-point lead held.

**Kingdom:** Lackeys, Secret Cave, Samurai, Storeroom, Ghost Town, Oasis, Apothecary, Devil's Workshop, University and Harbinger, plus the Event Sea Trade and the Project Sinister Plot. Everyone starts with a Magic Lamp, and the board uses Potions.

**Plan:** There was no strong trashing and no cheap village, so I played money plus two key cards. Devil's Workshop gains a Gold on any turn I buy nothing, and otherwise adds a second gain. Samurai gives +$1 every turn after it's played. I kept to it: 3 Devil's Workshops gave me 4 Golds and several free Silvers, then 2 Samurai, and I greened from 5 Provinces left. I never used Sea Trade.

**What decided it:** The bot's Apothecary deck reached Provinces earlier and led 21–15 on turn 15. My two Samurai then added $2 every turn, which made each later turn reliable. The extra gain from Devil's Workshop also bought points: a Duchy plus an Estate on turn 18, and the final Estate on turn 21. Without the Estate on turn 21, the bot's last Province would have ended the game 40–40.

**Tool problems:**
1. When a buy plays treasures for you, it plays only the ones needed and leaves the rest in hand. On turn 13 it showed "colonelpanic8 plays 4 Silvers. (+$8)" while my Gold stayed unplayed. With one Buy this costs nothing, but there's no way to say "play everything" in the same call. That would matter with +Buy, or with Magic Lamp, which counts the cards in play.
2. The opponent's zone text is hard to read: "hand+deck hold 6 of: 4 Copper, 2 Estate, Silver, 1 of them elsewhere" alongside "unlocated 4 Copper, 2 Estate, Silver".
3. The "Gain a card" decision from Devil's Workshop doesn't say which branch applied or what the cost cap is. I had to work that out from my own gain count.
4. Events and Projects (Sea Trade, Sinister Plot) are mixed into the buy list as if they were cards, with no type label.
5. To play a Night card I had to pick "End Buys (decline)" first, and nothing said that ending buys moves on to the Night phase.
