I won, 44 VP to 37. We each took 19 turns, and the game ended when I bought the last Province.

**Kingdom:** Messenger, Sack of Loot, Chariot Race, Clerk, Ghost Town, Artificer, Catacombs, Village, Hoard, Black Cat, plus Road Network (Project) and Way of the Horse (Way). Nothing on the board trashes cards, and the only village was plain Village, so I didn't build an engine.

**Plan:** Big Money with two Clerks, Sack of Loot and Gold, opening Silver/Clerk. I kept to it, except that I never bought the Black Cats I'd planned. A Cat would have been in my hand on only about one bot turn in six, so a flat Estate was worth more. Clerk paid off: it can be played free at the start of my turn, so two copies never collided.

**What decided it:** The bot drew $8–9 early and led 24 to 15 by turn 11. Two choices brought me back:
- **Turns 12 and 14:** I spent $10 on two Duchies rather than a Province plus a Black Cat. That kept 3 Provinces in the pile, and the longer game favoured my stronger deck (four Golds and Loot against its two Golds).
- **Turn 18:** I used Way of the Horse on a Clerk to reach $7, bought a Gold and put it on top of my deck with Insignia. The bot then took the second-last Province to tie at 37. That Gold helped me reach $13 the next turn for the last Province and an Estate.

**Problems with the tools:**
1. **Unlabelled options:** every Clerk start-of-turn decision had a mandatory `[reject-ability] Resolve an effect (choose 1)  5: 0  6: 1` group with no labels, plus a second `[way]` group. It wasn't clear what answering it would do.
2. **Buy list leaves out some treasures:** on turn 9 the header said "$7 available", but the buy list stopped at $4 because Puzzle Box wasn't counted.
3. **Cost shown as value:** the play list shows each treasure's cost, not what it makes ("Silver $3", "Puzzle Box $7"), which reads like the money it produces.
4. **Autoplay skipped Shield:** after "Autoplay Treasures" the log showed "plays a Copper and a Gold (+$4)" and left Shield, a plain +$3 +Buy treasure, in my hand.
5. **No Loot text:** `CARDS` gave "Loot Pile: no card text on record", so I couldn't value Sack of Loot until each Loot turned up.
