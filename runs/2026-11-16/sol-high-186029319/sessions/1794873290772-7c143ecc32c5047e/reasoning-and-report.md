- **Alternatives considered:** Witch-money starts scoring sooner but is vulnerable to junk and has a low scoring ceiling; a Treasure Map/Gold plan has explosive economy but unreliable setup and poor Curse handling. The engine has the strongest response to either and to its own mirror.
- **Openings:** **4/3: Temple/Silver**, aiming to remove Estates while reaching Witch; **5/2: Witch/Lackeys**, prioritizing attack tempo and immediate Villagers.
- **Build priorities:** First Witch, effective thinning, cheap draw and one Bauble; then enough villages for the actual terminal count. Green once output is reliable, generally at Province-plus-development or $13+ with two Buys, earlier if a pile finish threatens.
- **Ending/signals:** Track Curses, Lackeys and Cities for three-pile endings, alongside Provinces and Temple VP. Check Witch access after the second shuffle; if it is late, prioritize attacking/drawing over an elaborate build. As Curses near empty, favor Cities. If Lackeys becomes contested, secure draw before payload.
- **Challenged/least certain:** The main uncertainty is how fast Temple thinning survives opposing Witch tempo. A clean engine estimate is too optimistic under junking; if the deck stalls repeatedly, reduce the build ambition toward Witch-money and earlier points rather than collecting unsupported terminals.
